    How to play

    1 · Build

    Drag parts from the left onto the rocket; they snap into place. Put the Pilot Capsule or a light Probe Core on top. Tanks hold fuel, engines burn it. A Stage Separator splits the rocket into stages; press STAGE to drop the empty bottom part. Drag fins or struts onto a tank or engine.

    New parts

    Solid boosters (SRBs): fuel built in, lots of push, cheap, but once lit they burn at full power until empty: the throttle can't slow them. Side Boosters: drop the radial decoupler on a core tank to add a pair; select it to choose SRB or tank + engine and a nose cone. They fire with Stage 1, then STAGE drops them. Nose cones (optional) cut drag on top of the rocket or a booster. A Fairing goes under your payload and covers it; pick Ogive, Cone, Bullet or Bulbous. Press F to split it off in space.

    Many craft

    A Payload Separator goes under a satellite or lander: press R (Release) and it flies off as its own craft. A dropped stage with a probe core or capsule also becomes its own craft. Switch craft with [ and ] or the Vessels list (V). Probe core + Antenna + Solar Panels = a Satellite; command + engine + legs = a Lander. In Sandbox World choose Leave in space and your craft stay up there while you launch more. See them all in Tracking.

    2 · Fly

    Space / STAGE = start engines, then drop stages. W/S or ↑/↓ = throttle (Z max, X zero). A/D or ←/→ = turn. Or hold the mouse / trackpad on the screen to point that way. H = auto-point, M = map, P = parachute, G = landing legs, F = open fairing, , . = time warp (up to ×5000 when coasting in space), Esc = pause. Two-finger scroll zooms.

    3 · Orbit trick

    Fly up to about 8–10 km, then tip over sideways. Watch the Sideways speed climb toward Circle speed. When your low point (Pe) is above 30 km, you're in orbit!

    The math

    TWR = thrust ÷ weight. More than 1 means you can lift off.

    Δv ("delta-v") = how much speed your fuel can add. Δv = Isp × 9.81 × ln(full mass ÷ empty mass). Dropping empty stages makes the rest lighter, so you get more Δv!

    Parallel staging: core + side boosters burn together. Add the thrusts and the fuel flows: ṁ = thrust ÷ (Isp × 9.81). Mixed exhaust speed c = total thrust ÷ total ṁ, and Δv = c × ln(m start ÷ m end). When the boosters are empty, drop them: the lighter core then gets its own Δv.

    Drag = ½ ρ v² × drag factor. A blunt top is 1.6; a nose cone makes it 1.1–1.3; a closed fairing ≈ 0.9–1.4 depending on shape.

    Isp = how efficient an engine is (bigger is better). Engines are a bit weaker in thick air.

    Circle speed = √(μ ÷ r): how fast you must go sideways to keep falling around the planet instead of into it. On Numeria that's about 1,600 m/s.

    Ap / Pe = the highest and lowest points of your path.

    Soft landing: touch down under 10 m/s (12 on water). Parachutes help! Landing legs (G) let you land up to 12 m/s straight down, tilted up to 15°.

    Sphere of influence (SOI) = r = a × (m ÷ M)2/5: the bubble where a body's gravity is the one that counts. Inside Lumo's SOI (≈505 km) you orbit Lumo; outside Numeria's (≈11,900 km) you orbit the star Ember.
