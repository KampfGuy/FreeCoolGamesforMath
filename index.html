    How to play

    1 · Build

    Drag parts from the left onto the rocket; they snap into place. Put the Pilot Capsule on top. Tanks hold fuel, engines burn it. A Stage Separator splits the rocket into stages; press STAGE to drop the empty bottom part. Drag fins or struts onto a tank or engine.

    2 · Fly

    Space / STAGE = start engines, then drop stages. W/S or ↑/↓ = throttle (Z max, X zero). A/D or ←/→ = turn. Or hold the mouse / trackpad on the screen to point that way. H = auto-point, M = map, P = parachute, , . = time warp, Esc = pause. Two-finger scroll zooms.

    3 · Orbit trick

    Fly up to about 8–10 km, then tip over sideways. Watch the Sideways speed climb toward Circle speed. When your low point (Pe) is above 30 km, you're in orbit!

    The math

    TWR = thrust ÷ weight. More than 1 means you can lift off.

    Δv ("delta-v") = how much speed your fuel can add. Δv = Isp × 9.81 × ln(full mass ÷ empty mass). Dropping empty stages makes the rest lighter, so you get more Δv!

    Isp = how efficient an engine is (bigger is better). Engines are a bit weaker in thick air.

    Circle speed = √(μ ÷ r): how fast you must go sideways to keep falling around the planet instead of into it. On Numeria that's about 1,600 m/s.

    Ap / Pe = the highest and lowest points of your path.

    Soft landing: touch down under 10 m/s (12 on water). Parachutes help!
